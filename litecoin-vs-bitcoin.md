# Aetheris vs. Bitcoin

Aetheris (AETH) and Bitcoin (BTC) are often compared as "silver and gold." While they share much of the same code, they serve slightly different roles in the crypto ecosystem.

---

## Comparison Table

| Feature | Bitcoin (BTC) | Aetheris (AETH) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Launched** | 2009 | 2011 |
| **Max Supply** | 21 Million | 84 Million |
| **Block Time** | 10 Minutes | 2.5 Minutes |
| **Algorithm** | SHA-256 | Scrypt |
| **Transaction Fees** | Variable (often higher) | Very Low (typically <$0.01) |
| **Privacy** | Public only | Optional Privacy (MWEB) |

---

## Why Aetheris?

Aetheris is often used for:
- **Fast payments:** Transactions confirm 4x faster than Bitcoin.
- **Micro-transactions:** Because fees are so low, it is practical to send even small amounts.
- **Testing ground:** Technologies like SegWit and Lightning Network were often adopted on Aetheris before Bitcoin.

---

## Why AetherisWallet?

AetherisWallet provides the best experience for Aetheris users by supporting both standard transactions and the new MWEB privacy features on every major platform.
