# How to back up your AetherisWallet

**This is the single most important step in self-custody.** If you lose access to your device without a backup, your funds are gone forever. No one can recover them for you.

---

## The 12-Word Recovery Phrase

When you create an AetherisWallet wallet, the app generates a **12-word recovery phrase** (also called a seed phrase). This phrase is the master key to your funds.

- **Everything is backed up** — All your addresses, balances, and history are derived from these 12 words.
- **Never uploaded anywhere** — AetherisWallet stores it only on your device.
- **Cross-platform compatible** — You can use these same 12 words to restore your wallet on any platform AetherisWallet supports.

---

## How to find your phrase

On first setup, AetherisWallet shows your 12-word phrase automatically. If you skipped it, you can view it again:
1. Open AetherisWallet.
2. Go to **Settings** → **Security**.
3. Tap **View Recovery Phrase**.
4. Authenticate using your PIN or biometrics.

---

## Best practices for storage

### ✅ DO:
- **Write it down physically** on paper or engrave it in metal.
- **Store it in a safe place** like a fireproof safe or a bank deposit box.
- **Double-check the spelling** and the order of the words.

### ❌ DON'T:
- **Don't take a screenshot** or save it as a photo.
- **Don't save it in notes apps**, email, or cloud storage (Google Drive, iCloud).
- **Don't share it with anyone** — AetherisWallet support will NEVER ask for your recovery phrase.

---

## How to restore a wallet

If you get a new phone or lose access to your current one:
1. Install AetherisWallet on the new device.
2. Select **I already have a wallet** during setup.
3. Enter your 12 words in the correct order.
4. Your funds will appear once the wallet finishes syncing.
